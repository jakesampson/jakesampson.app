01 / A LITTLE ABOUT ME

Technical by nature.
Curious by default.

I’m a spatial developer and geospatial analyst interested in how software, GIS and location data can make complex systems easier to understand and work with.

Based in Brisbane, I’m interested in mapping and spatial challenges across South East Queensland, including the Gold Coast. I enjoy turning intricate data into useful tools, clear maps and dependable workflows.
